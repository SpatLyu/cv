\section{Some stuff about me}\label{some-stuff-about-me}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  My research interests lie in \textbf{advancing methodologies in
  spatial causal inference} and \textbf{developing high-performance
  computational tools}, with a primary focus on \emph{R packages}.
\item
  I specialize in \emph{data analysis}, \emph{statistical modeling}, and
  developing open-source analytical tools, including \emph{R packages},
  using \textbf{R}, \textbf{C++}, and \textbf{Python}, with a strong
  focus on \emph{spatial analysis}. I actively contribute to the R
  geospatial community and am dedicated to advancing open-source
  geospatial software.
\item
  Currently, my work centers on \textbf{Empirical Dynamic Modeling
  (EDM)} framework for modeling \emph{dynamic system},
  \textbf{information theory} for quantifying \emph{information flow}
  and \emph{variable interactions}, \textbf{ordinary differential
  equations (ODEs)} for characterizing \emph{spatiotemporal processes}
  and \emph{system evolution}, as well as \textbf{counterfactual} and
  \textbf{potential outcomes} framework for estimating \emph{causal
  effects}. I am particularly interested in leveraging these approaches
  to address critical challenges in \emph{urban sustainability},
  \emph{climate change mitigation}, and broader global issues.
\end{itemize}
